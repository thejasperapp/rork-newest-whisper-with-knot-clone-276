Title: Balcony Herb Plan

**Basil and Mint: Practical Setup Steps**

**1. Choosing Containers:**
- Use pots with drainage holes.
- Ensure pots are at least 12 inches deep for good root growth.

**2. Soil Preparation:**
- Use potting soil mixed with compost.
- Ensure soil is well-draining.

**3. Planting:**
- Plant basil and mint separately to prevent overcrowding.
- Space basil plants 12 inches apart.

**Watering and Maintenance:**

**4. Watering:**
- Water when the top inch of soil feels dry.
- Avoid waterlogging the soil.

**5. Harvesting:**
- Begin harvesting basil once the plant has 6-8 leaves.
- Pinch mint leaves regularly to encourage growth.

**Safety Note:**
- Keep mint in its own pot to prevent it from overtaking other plants.

With these steps, you can successfully cultivate basil and mint on your balcony, providing fresh herbs for your culinary needs while enjoying the fragrance they bring to your living space.
