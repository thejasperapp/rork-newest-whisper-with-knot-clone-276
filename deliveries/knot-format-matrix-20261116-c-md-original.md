# Balcony Herb Plan

## Basil

### Practical Setup Steps
1. **Container Selection**: Choose a container that is at least 6-8 inches deep. Ensure it has drainage holes to prevent waterlogging.
2. **Soil**: Use well-draining potting soil. Basil thrives in nutrient-rich environments.
3. **Placement**: Place the container in an area with full sun. Basil needs at least 6 hours of sunlight per day.

### Watering
- Water the basil plant regularly, keeping the soil moist but not waterlogged. Allow the top inch of soil to dry out between waterings.

### Harvesting
- Begin harvesting once the plant has at least 6-8 leaves. Pinch off leaves or cut stems just above a leaf node to encourage bushy growth.

## Mint

### Practical Setup Steps
1. **Container Selection**: Use a container with a depth of at least 8 inches with proper drainage systems.
2. **Soil**: Plant mint in a loamy, well-drained soil. It should retain moisture without becoming too soggy.
3. **Placement**: Position the pot in partial shade to avoid intense sunlight, which can scorch the plant.

### Watering
- Keep the soil consistently moist. Mint requires frequent watering, especially in warm weather, but avoid overwatering.

### Harvesting
- Start harvesting once the plant reaches about 6 inches in height. Frequent cutting helps promote growth.

### Safety Note
- **Containment**: Mint can be invasive and can quickly outgrow its space. It’s essential to keep it in a pot to prevent spreading.

## General Advice
- **Fertilization**: Apply a balanced, water-soluble fertilizer monthly during the growing season.
- **Pest Management**: Check regularly for pests such as aphids and use organic methods to control them.

This plan will help ensure a successful and manageable balcony herb garden. Regular maintenance and observation are key to thriving plants.
