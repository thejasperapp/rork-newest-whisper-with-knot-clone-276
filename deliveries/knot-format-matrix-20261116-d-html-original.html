Balcony Herb Plan

Basil

To grow basil on your balcony, follow these steps:

  • Choose a sunny spot that receives at least 6 hours of sunlight a day.
  • Use well-draining soil and a pot with drainage holes.
  • Water deeply but infrequently, allowing the soil to dry out between waterings.
  • Harvest basil leaves regularly to encourage growth.

Mint

To grow mint, follow these steps:

  • Mint thrives in partial shade and moist soil.
  • Use a separate pot to prevent mint from spreading too much.
  • Water frequently to keep the soil consistently damp.
  • Harvest mint leaves by pinching off stems just above a leaf pair.
Safety Note: Mint is known to be invasive. Ensure that it is contained in its own pot to prevent it from overtaking your balcony garden.

General Tips

  • Choose pots that fit your space and have good drainage.
  • Label each pot with the herb name and date planted.
  • Rotate your pots periodically to ensure even growth and sunlight exposure.
