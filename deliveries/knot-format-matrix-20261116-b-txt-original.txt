
Balcony Herb Plan
===================

Herbs: Basil and Mint

Practical Setup Steps:
1. Choose suitable containers with drainage holes.
2. Use quality potting soil and add compost.
3. Place containers where they receive 6-8 hours of sunlight per day.

Watering Advice:
- Basil: Water regularly. Keep the soil moist but not waterlogged.
- Mint: Water sparingly and ensure soil is well-drained. Mint prefers less water than basil.

Harvesting Advice:
- Basil: Begin harvesting when the plant has at least 6-8 leaves. Cut just above a leaf pair to encourage bushy growth.
- Mint: Harvest leaves regularly to encourage new growth. Pinch the top leaves as needed.

Safety Note:
- Mint can be invasive. Consider keeping it in a separate container to prevent it from spreading and taking over other plants.
