Balcony Herb Plan

Basil

Basil is a warm-weather herb that thrives in sunny conditions. Ideal for adding fresh flavor to a variety of dishes.

Setup Steps

  • Choose a container with drainage holes.
  • Fill with well-draining potting soil.
  • Position in a spot that receives 6-8 hours of sunlight.

Watering

Water deeply once a week. Ensure the soil remains moist but not waterlogged.

Harvesting

Start harvesting once the plant reaches about 6 inches. Pick leaves from the top to encourage bushy growth.

Mint

Mint is a versatile herb perfect for teas and garnishes. However, it can spread quickly and overpower other plants.

Setup Steps

  • Use a large container to prevent spread.
  • Fill with rich, well-draining soil.
  • Place in part shade, avoiding harsh midday sun.

Watering

Keep the soil consistently moist. Mint prefers regular watering.

Harvesting

Begin harvesting as soon as the plant is established. Regular pruning encourages new growth.

Safety Note

Keep mint contained to prevent it from spreading. Consider using a separate container if planting with other herbs.
